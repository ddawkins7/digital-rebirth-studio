Privacy Policy

Digital Rebirth N8N · Last updated: September 26, 2026

Who this covers

Digital Rebirth N8N is a private, self-hosted automation tool operated by Digital Rebirth Studio for its own use. The only Google account that connects to it is the owner's.

What Google data it uses, and why

  • YouTube: to upload videos to, and read basic details of, the owner's own channels.
  • Google Sheets: to read and update the owner's own production queues and logs.
  • Google Drive: to store and read the owner's own files.

The tool does not access any data beyond what these tasks need.

Storage and sharing

Access tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's private server. Google user data is not sold, not shared with third parties, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI models.

Digital Rebirth N8N's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
