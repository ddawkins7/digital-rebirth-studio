Digital Rebirth N8N

Digital Rebirth N8N is the studio's private, self-hosted automation tool. It runs only for the studio and connects only to the studio's own Google account, where it:

  • uploads finished videos to the studio's own YouTube channels;
  • reads and updates the studio's own Google Sheets (production queues and logs);
  • stores and reads files in the studio's own Google Drive.

It is not offered to the public. See the privacy policy for how Google data is handled.
